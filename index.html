Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if my event date is available?

Simply use our Date Availability Tool above or call us at (877) 338-6037. We verify availability instantly so you don't wait days for an answer.

How does the Online Music Jukebox and planning system work?

Once booked, you receive password-protected access to our online client portal. You can submit song requests ("Must Play", "Do Not Play"), set up announcement details for your MC, and refine your timeline step-by-step.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes! While our primary areas are New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Lafayette, and South Louisiana, we regularly serve destination weddings. Our streamlined planning system makes remote coordination simple.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. Geaux Live is fully insured. If your venue requires a Certificate of Insurance (COI) prior to your event, we provide it directly to venue management upon request.
